Chrono settings

Projectile

Sets the drag model used to work back to muzzle speed. Changing it starts a new string.

Report speed

Muzzle speed is estimated from the speed track. "At the chrono" is the speed where the projectile passes the radar.

Units
Sensitivity

Higher catches weaker echoes (foam darts) but is more likely to trigger on things moving nearby.

Mounting

Stand the chrono a few feet in front of the muzzle, aimed back at it, a little below the line of fire.

How the phone connects

  1. Turn the chrono on. It starts its own Wi-Fi network, CHRONO-7F2A.
  2. On the iPhone, open Settings, then Wi-Fi, and join it. It will say No Internet Connection. That is expected.
  3. Open Safari to 192.168.4.1 (or chrono.local), or add it to your Home Screen once. No app, internet or cell service needed.
